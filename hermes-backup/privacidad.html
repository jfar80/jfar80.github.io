Privacy policy (English)

  • It only accesses files it creates itself in its owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope). It cannot see any other file.
  • Everything is encrypted before upload.
  • It does not collect, sell or share anyone's data.
  • It has no other users: only its owner uses it.

Contact: @jfar80 on GitHub.
